# [Overwatch] Glitch to unfairly unlock 'The Floor is Lava' achievement with Lúcio

## Game Description
Overwatch is a team-based 6v6 hero shooter game. Test conducted on the launch version (6v6) for consoles and PC.

## Severity / Priority
* **Severity:** Medium (Allows unfair achievement/trophy earning and may encourage unsportsmanlike behavior during casual matches).
* **Priority:** Medium (Relies on a specific matchmaking flow and the spontaneous departure of an opposing player).

## Status

🟢 **Fixed in 2017**

## Test Environment
* **Platform:** Xbox One Fat (Bug replicable on PlayStation 4 and PC).
* **Version/Year:** Found in 2017.
* **Game Mode:** Quick Play / Casual.

## Prerequisites
* Select the hero **Lúcio** (applicable to other heroes such as Roadhog, Symmetra, and Winston).
* Be in a group/external voice chat with at least 1 friend (Discord, Skype, Console Party).
* The selected map must contain environmental hazards/chasms (e.g., Numbani).

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Join a Quick Play / Casual match.
2. During the match, spot a player leaving the opposing team.
3. Ask your friend to access your profile in the console/game menu and select **"Join Game"** to fill the vacant spot on the rival team.
4. In the following round, select the hero Lúcio and meet your friend at an isolated spot on the map.
5. Fulfill the achievement requirement(s): eliminate the opponent (friend) 3 times in a row while wall-riding, without dying in between eliminations.

## Expected Result
The Matchmaking system should not allow players from the same friends list to fill open spots on the opposing team in public matches, preventing match manipulation and achievement farming.

## Actual Result
The system allows a friend to join the opposing team via "Join Game", enabling stats manipulation and effortless achievement unlocks without detection from anti-cheat or anti-exploit systems.
